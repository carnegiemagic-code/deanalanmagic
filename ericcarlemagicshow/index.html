EricCarleMagicShow

A Charming Preschool Magic Show That Celebrates The Books & Stories of Eric Carle

Remember The Very Hungry Caterpillar? Well, the man that wrote and illustrated that wonderful story was Eric Carle. I’ve taken some of his  stories and added magic to them so that the stories come to life in a most unique way.

In my show I cover, The Very Busy Spider, The Tiny Seed, The Grouch Lady Bug and of course, The Very Hungry Caterpillar. Plus I’m always adding things to the show, so you’ll never know which book or story I’ll surprise you with next?!!!

I usually do the show in February, but it will be available all year, with the exception of Summer.
